# v0.2.7 embedded owner scope decisions

October 6, 2026. The owner accepted decisions 4 and 6: strict read-only
identity and existing reporting are sufficient for the RLVGL scope; temperature
validation gates only the specific CCPS functions that depend on it. CCPS
prototype wiring divergences are intentional ease-of-build adaptations with
matched firmware/hardware, including ESP32 routing revision changes. They are
not generic RLVGL release blockers; later firmware revisions may retire old rigs.

## Normative amendment and compatibility bridge

The dedicated [KI2C errata log](../concepts/KI2C-ERRATA.md) was activated from
the current SBC template pinned at parent commit
`ddafeb3ffb20ab4ff924b925d88ca47577f2bafb`, before the scope amendment.
ERRATA-001 records the real read-only/configuration-dependent temperature
conflict and owner disposition. It remains diagnosed pending a resolving commit;
no physical verification or historical issues are invented.

[KI2C-07 §15 revision 0.1.0](../concepts/KI2C-07-HARDWARE-CONFORMANCE.md)
records the owner-ratified scope amendment, dated October 6 with `Change kind: scope`,
`AmendmentStatus: amended` in its heading and an explicit rationale, before
requirements were changed. The phase remains hardware-blocked; owner acceptance
of this scope does not close its physical gates. Legacy §12 remains
byte-for-byte unchanged. The current-SBC bridge does not re-ratify its old
physical observations or fabricate a conformance run.

The operative read-only profile now performs STTS22H and VEML3235SL typed
identity probes without required temperature/ALS capture or configuration writes.
PTN3460 configuration validity and PCM3168A reset-state probes remain **health
results**, not silicon-identity tests. Existing logical-bus/electrical/clock/reset
prerequisites and board-owned `HardwareReady` remain mandatory for physical
access. EEPROM U6 and KSZ9897S remain unadmitted.

The existing §7 record and §3 result vocabulary are retained as they stand.
Operations and errors/skips must be named in that record; temperature capture
is unattempted in this profile. The proposed `ki2c-conformance/v1` is not adopted.
Identity or health success does not establish temperature validation, electrical
readiness or board qualification. No physical checklist box was closed.

## CCPS prototype and release boundary

Owner acceptance of matched prototype revisions does not prescribe one pin map
or require every old rig to converge before publishing generic RLVGL packages.
Affected CCPS functions still require their own actual temperature validation
and correctly matched firmware/hardware/routing before qualification. Retirement
of older rigs belongs to subsequent firmware revisions.

The August 29 archival observations, source-row hashes, NVS history and fresh
C3/H747 software image receipts remain preserved in
[the amended embedded next-slice receipt](v0.2.7-embedded-next-slice.md).
They are historical or candidate evidence, not a new measurement. The scope
decision does not alter the parent Rung 0 activation-contact hold, schematic
no-connects, current recorder custody, DE/RE state, battery access or production
hardware documentation. No universal production contact map is adopted here.

Missing temperature/prototype-wiring proof is no longer a generic RLVGL release
blocker. Packaging, publication, consumer and applicable phase gates remain
independent requirements; this scope amendment does not pass them.
