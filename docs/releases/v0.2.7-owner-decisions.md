## Scope of remaining gates

RLVGL publication still requires the staging correction, all applicable
prepublish checks, complete Gate P and packaged consumers, registry ownership
and publisher setup, exact source publication and parent-gitlink verification.
An owner decision is not registry or consumer proof.

KI2C identity observations must retain device, bus and error classifications.
PTN configuration magic and PCM reset-state observations are health results,
not silicon identity. A skipped or blocked temperature operation is reported
as such; no configuration write or weakened coherence guard follows from the
identity-only decision. Board electrical/topology prerequisites continue to
bound any physical board-conformance claim.

CCPS temperature-dependent functions and revision-specific physical tests carry
their own validation gates. They do not propagate into the portable RLVGL
library release merely because their firmware consumes RLVGL. The wiring
clarification does not select a new pin map or replace historical evidence.

## Parallel work ownership

Three existing agents handle MPY disposition preparation, the KI2C amendment
and CCPS scope correction, and the producer-side repair respectively. The
coordinator owns shared release documentation, family registration, index
regeneration and patch preservation. Original parent and submodule WIP remain
untouched. No board access, deployment, registry upload or parent adoption is
part of these decision updates.
