## Preparation outcome and stop packet

The [software preparation packet](v0.2.7-ki2c-software-preparation.md) supplies
a command/example boundary and proposed versioned conformance-record shape.
It remains design-only: no binary, schema validator, controller access, device
protocol, configuration write or physical observation was added.

One concrete discrepancy needs owner disposition before the temperature
capture step can be implemented. KI2C-07 §6 requests a coherent STTS22H read
without changing configuration. KI2C-02 §7 and the accepted driver require
successful `configure` to establish local BDU/auto-increment state first.
A newly constructed driver therefore returns `NotConfigured` without a bus
transaction. No accepted API imports an externally observed configuration.
The design retains that step as prerequisite-blocked, preserves the identity
probe, and changes neither contract. No decision or amendment is recorded here.

Physical KI2C-07 remains blocked pending identified board/revision/operator,
source/application/image/device-tree identities, all three actual physical
controller mappings and permission, `TEMP_ADDR` high-impedance evidence,
PL pull-up-voltage/input-threshold assessment, and observed PCM rail/reset/
synchronous-clock readiness. Successful mock transactions and Linux builds
cannot supply those facts. PTN3460 and PCM3168A probes remain health checks,
not silicon identity claims.

EEPROM U6 and KSZ9897S remain evidence-gated and excluded. Configuration,
flash/EDID/link operations, audio streaming, broader codec controls and stronger
bus-sharing modes remain outside the accepted slice. A future implementation
must retain the Qwen executor/Llama judge and human-review discipline; these
test reruns do not substitute for it. No phase or initiative closure, commit,
push, tag or publication occurred.
