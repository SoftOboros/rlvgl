## Remaining boundaries and decision stop

- **PCDN-MPY-04-017 remains proposal-only.** Canonical Batch-frame lowering
  into `BatchStageDirection` and owned wire Result publication are not closed.
  Existing typed in-process batches, native completions and allocation-free
  operation codecs do not prove that complete ingress/egress path. No further
  dependent implementation proceeded under this receipt.
- Snapshot subscription metadata and canonical byte encoding/cross-driver
  equivalence remain open. Deterministic Rust record ordering and explicit
  truncation are narrower evidence than a byte-equivalence claim.
- Result-bearing actions, complete director-facing statistics/capability
  discovery, and full Endpoint command/binding projection remain open.
- Actual callable retention/execution/finalization, callback-generated
  command deferral and VM-safe behavior remain MPY-06 evidence gates. Integer
  Callback IDs and queued release notices do not qualify real VM execution.
- MPY-06/07 remain owner-accepted but unratified; no new binding or same-core
  conformance behavior is authorized by these test results.
- MPY-08/09 remain Draft. Physical dual-core placement/coherency, transport,
  flow control, performance budgets and release closure remain separately gated.

A decision packet for MPY-04-017 must identify canonical decoding and ordered
lowering, owned lifetime-safe Result fields, bounded admission/capacity,
operation/field-attributed failure, and the exact allowed shared paths. These
are review topics, not newly accepted semantics. Future shared runtime edits
also require a new CPY-02 handoff with explicit path authority.
